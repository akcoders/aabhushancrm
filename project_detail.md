# Kalasha Fine Jewels CRM

## Complete Project, Product, Functional, and Technical Documentation

**Project type:** Jewellery Customer Relationship Management System
**Brand:** Kalasha Fine Jewels
**Primary purpose:** Lead conversion, customer relationship management, sales tracking, loyalty, retention, exhibition management, staff accountability, and repeat-business growth
**Application style:** Premium jewellery showroom CRM with role-based access, intelligent recommendations, and long-term customer memory

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Kalasha Fine Jewels CRM is a complete customer relationship and showroom management platform designed specifically for jewellery businesses.

Unlike a general-purpose CRM, it understands that jewellery buying is a long and relationship-driven journey. A customer may first meet the brand through Instagram, visit an exhibition, ask about a bridal collection, compare designs with family, return after several weeks, place a custom order, and purchase again during a festival or anniversary.

The CRM connects all these interactions into one continuous customer journey.

It enables the business to:

- Capture every enquiry and showroom visitor.
- Assign every lead to a responsible salesperson.
- Track follow-ups and identify overdue work.
- Measure salesperson conversion performance.
- Convert leads into permanent customer profiles.
- Record sales, payments, jewellery specifications, and commissions.
- Manage custom jewellery orders from design to delivery.
- Recognize returning exhibition visitors.
- Track exhibition revenue, expense, profit, and ROI.
- Segment customers for targeted marketing.
- Generate retention opportunities using customer dates and purchase history.
- Reward customers through loyalty points, gift cards, and privilege cards.
- Help staff understand whom to contact, why to contact them, and what to say.
- Give owners a measurable view of marketing quality, staff performance, and customer value.

The core philosophy is simple:

> Every customer interaction should become reusable business intelligence.

## 2. The Business Problem

Jewellery businesses often manage customer relationships through notebooks, spreadsheets, personal WhatsApp chats, staff memory, and billing software.

This creates several problems:

- Leads are collected but not followed up consistently.
- Customer information remains with individual salespeople.
- Owners cannot identify which lead source produces valuable customers.
- Exhibition visitors are forgotten after the event.
- Returning visitors are treated like new visitors.
- Staff do not know which customer should be contacted next.
- Birthdays, anniversaries, weddings, and important family dates are missed.
- Old and inactive customers are not systematically recovered.
- Customer tastes, budget, and purchase intentions are not remembered.
- Loyalty points and gift-card balances are difficult to communicate.
- Custom-order delays are discovered too late.
- Pending payments may not receive timely attention.
- Salesperson performance is measured only by final sales, not by lead conversion and follow-up quality.
- Marketing campaigns are sent to broad lists instead of relevant segments.
- The owner cannot connect campaigns or exhibitions to actual revenue.

Kalasha Fine Jewels CRM solves these problems by creating one shared and structured source of truth.

## 3. Product Vision

The CRM is designed to become the digital memory and daily operating system of the jewellery showroom.

It supports the complete customer lifecycle:

```text
Marketing Source / Walk-in / Exhibition
                  ↓
                Lead
                  ↓
       Salesperson Assignment
                  ↓
   Interest, Budget, Occasion, Notes
                  ↓
       Follow-ups and Communication
                  ↓
          Lead Conversion
                  ↓
        Customer 360° Profile
                  ↓
    Sale / Custom Order / Payment
                  ↓
 Loyalty / Gift Card / Privilege Card
                  ↓
 Retention / Occasion / Cleaning Reminder
                  ↓
      Repeat Visit and Repeat Sale
```

The system does not treat a sale as the end of the journey. A completed sale becomes the starting point for future service, loyalty, relationship-building, and repeat business.

## 7. Salesperson Assignment and Performance

Salesperson accountability is a major feature of the CRM.

### Lead assignment

- A salesperson is required when a lead is created or edited.
- The salesperson appears directly in the lead grid.
- Leads can be filtered by salesperson.
- The salesperson is shown on the lead profile.
- Exhibition leads can receive automatic assignment.

### Salesperson performance metrics

For each salesperson, the CRM calculates:

- Assigned leads
- Open leads
- Converted leads
- Conversion rate
- Upcoming pending follow-ups
- Overdue follow-ups
- Completed follow-ups
- Sales count
- Generated sales revenue

### Conversion-rate logic

```text
Conversion Rate =
Converted Leads ÷ Total Assigned Leads × 100
```

Example:

```text
Assigned leads: 40
Converted leads: 12
Conversion rate: 30%
```

### Why this is useful

It prevents performance measurement from depending only on total sales.

The manager can identify:

- Who converts leads most effectively
- Who has too many open leads
- Who is maintaining follow-up discipline
- Who has overdue work
- Who generates the highest revenue
- Who may need coaching or redistribution of leads

## 19. Retention Engine

The retention engine identifies reasons to contact existing customers.

It is a deterministic business-rules engine. It does not depend on external generative AI.

### Retention opportunities

- Birthday
- Anniversary
- Family birthday
- Family anniversary
- Custom important date
- Jewellery cleaning
- Inactive customer
- Win-back
- VIP relationship
- Loyalty balance
- Gift-card expiry
- Festival campaign

### Important-date logic

The system calculates the next annual occurrence of:

- Birthday
- Anniversary
- Family date
- Custom important date

It then checks whether the date falls within the configured upcoming window.

### Win-back logic

- Six months without a purchase: win-back opportunity
- Twelve months without a purchase: inactive customer
- VIP/HNI customers receive higher relationship priority

### Jewellery-cleaning logic

Sales can generate cleaning reminders after:

- Six months
- Twelve months

The reminder uses the purchased jewellery type to make the message relevant.

### Loyalty logic

Customers with available loyalty points and no recent purchase can receive a reminder.

### Gift-card logic

Customers with an active gift card, remaining balance, and approaching expiry can receive a reminder.

### Deduplication

Before creating a retention message, the system checks whether a matching active message already exists.

This prevents duplicate reminders from being generated every time the engine runs.

### Retention output

The engine can create:

- Retention score
- Reason
- Suggested product
- Suggested offer
- Personalized message
- WhatsApp link
- Assigned staff member
- Smart task

It is rules-based and explainable.

Every generated task contains:

- Task code
- Task type
- Title
- Reason
- Priority

- Due date
- Related customer, lead, sale, order, or exhibition
- Suggested action
- Suggested product
- Suggested offer
- Call script
- WhatsApp message
- WhatsApp URL

### Lead rules

- Exhibition lead not contacted within 24 hours
- Hot lead without follow-up for two days
- Warm lead without follow-up for five days
- Cold lead ready for a soft follow-up
- New lead uncontacted within 24 hours

### Customer rules

- Upcoming birthday
- Upcoming anniversary
- Family birthday
- Six-month inactivity
- Twelve-month inactivity
- VIP/HNI inactivity

### Sale rules

- Thank-you call after purchase

- Partial payment follow-up
- High-value purchase relationship call

### Custom-order rules

- Confirm requirements
- Obtain design approval

### Loyalty and gift-card rules

## 32. Example Business Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Instagram bridal lead

1. Lead enters from Instagram.
2. Manager assigns a salesperson.
3. Salesperson records bridal interest and budget.
4. Lead is marked Hot.
5. Follow-up is scheduled.
6. If not contacted, an urgent smart task is generated.
7. Customer visits the showroom.
8. Lead is converted.
9. Sale or custom order is created.
10. Loyalty points are earned.
11. Future bridal and anniversary campaigns can reuse the profile.

### Scenario 2: Returning exhibition visitor

1. Visitor submits details at Exhibition A.
2. CRM creates a lead and event-visit record.
3. The same visitor attends Exhibition B.
4. Mobile matching identifies the person.
5. A duplicate lead is not created.
6. Visit count and interest snapshot are updated.
7. Staff sees previous preferences and transaction history.
8. The customer receives a contextual conversation.

### Scenario 3: Six-month cleaning reminder

1. Customer buys a necklace.
2. Sale and item details are stored.
3. CRM calculates the six-month cleaning date.
4. Retention scan creates a cleaning message.
5. Smart task is assigned to the customer’s salesperson.
6. Staff opens WhatsApp and contacts the customer.
7. Customer revisits the showroom.
8. New interest or purchase can be recorded.

### Scenario 4: Gift-card expiry

1. Customer has an active gift card.
2. Remaining balance is detected.
3. Expiry falls within the reminder window.
4. CRM generates an urgent or high-priority task.
5. Message includes the available balance.
6. Customer is invited to redeem it before expiry.

### Scenario 5: Privilege membership

1. A VIP or HNI customer is selected.
2. Staff issues a Diamond Privilege Card.
3. CRM generates a unique 16-digit card number.
4. Amount, validity, and membership status are stored.
5. The premium card appears in the grid.
6. The same card is visible on the customer profile.
7. Staff can immediately recognize the customer’s service level.

### Scenario 6: Sales-manager review

1. Manager opens the dashboard.
2. Team conversion ranking is reviewed.
3. One salesperson has many assigned leads but low conversion.
4. Another salesperson has multiple overdue follow-ups.
5. Manager redistributes work or provides coaching.
6. Improvement can be measured using the same metrics.

## 38. Current Implementation Boundaries

For an accurate presentation, the following points should be communicated honestly:

- WhatsApp messages currently use generated `wa.me` links; a WhatsApp Business provider is not yet connected.
- Email and SMS delivery providers are not yet connected.
- Campaign launch prepares and tracks recipient records but is not yet a production bulk-message delivery queue.
- PDF invoices are not yet implemented.
- Excel/PDF report exports can be added.
- Cloud attachment and custom-design image upload can be improved.
- The live local CRM uses the MySQL connection. Automated tests continue to use an isolated in-memory SQLite database so test runs never modify business records.
- Privilege Card value is currently membership information, not a redeemable transaction wallet.
- Privilege Card benefit rules can be added as a future enhancement.

These are integration and expansion opportunities rather than limitations of the core CRM concept.
